Benchmark: Visual multi-session SLAM under lighting, season and scene change

CROSS gives the best visual multi-session relocalization in this benchmark: from a cold start in a map from another session, it finds its place far more often than open-source SLAM systems run on the same sessions. It also ranks first in localization and mapping.

The figures show CROSS with a stereo camera and odometry (stereo_wheel), the recommended mode for these sensors. RTAB-Map is the other system that takes the same odometry: wheel odometry on OpenLORIS, close to the ground truth elsewhere (INS velocities on KITTI, ground truth plus noise on ROVER and SimChange). What we measure

Three tasks, and how every system is run

Three tasks under one protocol: the same recordings and radii for every system, and the same odometry for every system that takes it.

  1. T3 Relocalization

    Can it find its place from a cold start?

    Each query session is cut into 10 s trials: 100 frames at 10 Hz, a new trial every 50 frames. A trial loads the stored map and starts without knowing its pose. It succeeds when its final pose lies within the radius of the ground truth.

    • Indoors1 m · 2 m
    • Outdoors3 m · 5 m

  2. T2 Localization in a stored map

    How much of a query session is localized?

    A query session (another recording of the same place) runs once against the stored map, from its first frame and with no initial pose. Recall is the share of its frames that have a pose in the map within the radius; a frame without one counts as a miss.

    • Indoors1 m · 2 m
    • Outdoors3 m · 5 m

  3. T1 Mapping accuracy

    How accurate is the map of one session?

    One session is mapped from scratch. The error is the RMSE of the final trajectory after loop closure (ATE), aligned to the ground truth and divided by the length of the path. For CROSS the trajectory is its map keyframes, which are sparser than the other systems' keyframes.

    • DatasetsKITTI, OpenLORIS, ROVER
    • Failure< 80% of frames covered

Each dataset weighs the same in an overall score: the mean of OpenLORIS, ROVER and SimChange v3 for localization and relocalization (v3 replaced v2 on 25 October 2026; v2's tables are in the full results), and of KITTI, OpenLORIS and ROVER for mapping, where the error per path length lets kilometer drives and room-scale sessions compare. Localization and relocalization score only the frames that the map covers and in which the robot moves. Bold: the radius of the NeurIPS 2026 paper, in large type in the tables.

How every system is run

  • Same inputs

    Every system gets the same images at 10 Hz in real-time order. Every system that takes odometry gets the same stream: the dataset's odometry or a Basalt stereo VIO (OpenLORIS keeps its wheel odometry in the VIO rows).

  • Shipped defaults

    Each system runs its published defaults for the sensor, with no per-dataset tuning; the protocol lists the declared deviations. One is not listed there: outdoors (KITTI, ROVER), the CROSS row with Depth Anything 3 and visual odometry uses a different odometry-noise setting.

  • Map reuse

    CROSS saves and loads its map, ORB-SLAM3 loads and merges its atlas, and RTAB-Map loads its database in localization mode. MASt3R-SLAM, VGGT-SLAM 2.0, LingBot-Map and AMB3R-SLAM cannot store a map, so the map session and the query run as one stream, which keeps the map session's live state.

Datasets

4 datasets, 86 sessions, 2,205 relocalization trials

Real robots and rendered scenes, revisited under change: the same places at night, in another season, with people about and with the furniture moved.

ROVER

An outdoor robot on one campus route, over fourteen months

  • Size8 sessions · 1,137 relocalization trials
  • Changesseason, weather, dusk and night
  • Odometrysimulated from the ground truth plus noise (the robot has no wheel odometry)
  • Tasksmapping, localization, relocalization

An indoor robot in an office, a corridor, a home, a café and a supermarket

  • Odometrythe robot's wheel odometry
  • Tasksmapping, localization, relocalization

Rendered scenes with camera noise and blur: a house, a restaurant and a lab corridor (photos: the v2 renders)

  • Odometrysimulated from the ground truth plus noise
  • Taskslocalization, relocalization

A car on town streets, country roads and a highway around Karlsruhe

How each one works, and how it reuses a map

CROSS against two classical feature-based systems and four learned feed-forward ones, all open source.

SystemHow it worksSensorsMap reuse

This project

  • CROSS

    jiaming-ai/CROSS ↗

    Long-term relocalization with a probabilistic topological map: a keyframe graph with verified loop closures, and the camera pose tracked as a mixture of hypotheses until the evidence separates them. Relative poses come from VGGT-Omega (with any camera), PnP on depth (RGB-D) or the Depth Anything 3 back end (mono). MIT-licensed code; the VGGT-Omega and Depth Anything 3 weights are for non-commercial use only, and the RGB-D modes with PnP use neither.

    Sensors
    RGB-D, stereo or mono · camera only, + IMU or + odometry
    Map reuse
    saves and loads its map

Classical, feature-based

    UZ-SLAMLab/ORB_SLAM3 ↗

    Visual and visual-inertial SLAM on ORB features, with a multi-map atlas.

    Map reuse
    loads and merges its atlas

    loads its database in localization mode
