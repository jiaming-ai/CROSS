How it is built

Keyframe images and poses come from the saved map; depth from the stereo pair (SGBM) or the RGB-D sensor initialises one Gaussian per voxel. Large maps split into chunks of up to 200 keyframes on the ground plane, each trained independently with gsplat (MCMC densification, depth loss, per-keyframe pose and exposure refinement). Each chunk keeps its own cell; every Gaussian is anchored to its four nearest keyframes, so a re-optimised map moves the reconstruction without retraining.
